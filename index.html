About

Pantnagar Green Trails is a demonstration prototype for GBPUAT Pantnagar that links campus trees, QR-code learning and sustainable bicycle use.

Tree profiles are editable sample information and should be verified by campus botanists before real use. The Gulmohar profile is adapted from the supplied field guide.

No payments are processed here. Payment details are submitted separately through a Google Form, and nothing on this site confirms payment.
